Whoops! I forgot!

A Bit Late LLC submits assignments late sometimes. This is because the owner of the company had a wifi outage at his location, followed by a subsequent breaking down of his car, leaving him unable to even travel to the library with a flash drive to complete the assignment.

Uh-Oh! What happens now?

Luckily, the owner of the parent company, Forgiving Professor Corp. has graciously extended an offer to allow A Bit Late LLC to submit an assignment way too late for only a good few points off!

This allows A Bit Late LLC to complete the assignment despite already being past the due date, earning it its name. However, A Bit Late LLC does not intend to continue failing to submit assignments on time. It actually means it this time, it just needs to buy a domain.

Stay tuned for next episode where our hero once again fails to achieve its next form: Actually On Time LLC!
